About Matthew

Local roots, hands-on care.

Born in the Adirondacks and raised on its lakes and trails, Matthew Nothdurft is a licensed real estate broker with a deep love for the region he calls home. He covers the Lake George and Schroon Lake regions of New York.

What sets him apart is a hands-on, personal approach. From routine check-ins to coordinating repairs with the right contractors and vendors, he treats every property like his own.

Areas served

The Adirondack region of New York

  • Lake George

    Lakeside homes, seasonal cottages, and year-round properties around the lake and its villages.

  • Schroon Lake

    Quiet shoreline, mountain views, and classic Adirondack camps in the Schroon Lake area.

  • Queensbury

    Year-round living close to Glens Falls, with easy access to the lakes and the Northway.

  • The greater Adirondacks

    Woods, lakes, and small towns across the Adirondack region of New York.
